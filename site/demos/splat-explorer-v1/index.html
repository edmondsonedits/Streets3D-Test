Camera-height lock

ON: move sideways and forward while staying on the selected capture-level plane. Looking up or down does not change your height. OFF: fly freely, including Q/E for vertical movement. Selecting another capture view resets the reference level. Views are kept upright for exploration; original camera roll is not reproduced.

Browser rendering: Spark 2.3.1. It may differ from LichtFeld's native GUT renderer. Web copies retain all splats and available SH bands, but compression can change fine detail. No collision or surveyed road height is implied.
